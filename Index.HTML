V1 REFLECTION

The tests could only prove one rule at a time; V1 runs them together as one system that actually moves and responds to the real world. The collective drift feels most like the intended piece — one body, reacting to something outside itself, with no controls to hide behind. It drifts generic in the bounce: reflecting off walls reads as a physics demo, not a gesture with intent. In v2, the one change is the bounce rule — replacing the flat reflection with a turn shaped by the swarm's own atmosphere, not by the edge of the screen.
