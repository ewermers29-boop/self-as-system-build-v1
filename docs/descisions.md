# V1 Decisions

## Keep Two

### 1. Repeatable Sigil
I am keeping the 10 identical ant-like creatures because their simple, repeatable form stayed clear and recognizable when tested.

### 2. Shared Movement
I am keeping the shared movement because having all 10 creatures move together makes them feel like one connected swarm.

## Cut One

### Brightness Control
I am cutting the brightness control because I found that temperature creates a stronger connection between the environment and the swarm's movement.

## Tighten One

### Temperature
I am tightening the temperature rule so the swarm's speed changes within a specific temperature range, with the swarm stopping at 36°F and reaching its fastest speed at 100°F.

## Ask One

### Temperature and Behavior
I want to find out how clearly changing the temperature can control the movement of the entire swarm while keeping it feeling like one system.
